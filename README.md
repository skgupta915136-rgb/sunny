# 🌸 सारा AI (Sara AI) - Multilingual Voice & Multimodal Personal Assistant

> **Created and Engineered with Care by Mister Sunny Pannalal Gupta ji (मिस्टर सन्नी पन्नालाल गुप्ता जी)**

Sara AI is a state-of-the-art multilingual personal AI assistant featuring real-time speech recognition, sweet Indian female voice synthesis, multimodal camera vision understanding, persistent memory, and live contextual data.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🎙️ Real-Time Voice Interaction:** Natural voice conversation in 16+ Indian regional languages (Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, etc.) with locked, consistent Indian female voice.
- **📷 Multimodal Camera & Vision:** Live camera scanning and photo upload to read text/OCR, solve math problems, identify objects, and explain complex subjects in simple language.
- **🧠 Personal Memory & Profile:** Remembers user profile details and preferences across sessions.
- **⚡ Real-Time Context:** Always aware of live Indian Standard Time (IST), exact date, day of week, and city weather.
- **💬 Persistent Chat History:** Fully searchable local chat history, 1-click text copy, WhatsApp & SMS sharing, and `.txt` file export.
- **🎛️ Voice Control & Customization:** Rate, pitch, voice preview, and voice locking.
- **🎤 Presentation & Speech:** Ready-to-read speech script accessible in `SARA_AI_PRESENTATION_SPEECH.md` and directly in the app's Settings menu.

---
